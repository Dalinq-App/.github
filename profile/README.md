Your friend is on Apple Music. You're on Spotify. The link you send never opens right.

**Dalinq fixes that.** Share a song from your music app, Dalinq turns it into one link, and it opens in whatever app your friend actually uses.

**Works with** Spotify · Apple Music · YouTube Music · Deezer · Tidal

- Built into your phone's share sheet: two taps, no copy-paste
- Send songs straight to friends, see when they open them
- Badges, levels and a weekly leaderboard with your friends
- Free. No subscriptions, no ads.
